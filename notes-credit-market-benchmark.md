## 3. Design patterns worth copying

1. **Peg the credit to dollars, not to an invented token.** Perplexity publishes **100 credits = $1**, so Max's 10,000 monthly credits are legible as $100 of agent work and the Max spending cap ($200 default, up to $5,000) is readable in real money.
2. **Publish a flat per-action rate card for the cheap, high-frequency modalities.** OpenAI: image **5 credits/generation**, deep research **50 credits/task**, agent mode **30 credits/message**, voice **1.25 credits/minute**.
3. **Do not meter the thing you most want people to do.** ChatGPT keeps everyday text chat unlimited (guardrailed) while metering images, voice, research and agents; Perplexity's Ask searches consume **no credits** at all.
4. **Make expensive-but-light work free, and say so.** ChatGPT: "if ChatGPT automatically routes your message to a mini version of a model, **0 credits** are used"; Gemini: Flash-Lite prompts are free, failed requests are never charged, and Veo 3.1 Lite is **0 credits** for Ultra subscribers and family managers.
5. **One fungible pool across products instead of per-product caps.** Grok spends **one shared weekly pool** across Chat, Imagine, Voice, Build and API; ChatGPT credits bought in Codex can fund Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
6. **Pool credits at the tenant, not the seat.** Copilot Credits are pooled tenant-wide; ChatGPT Business runs a shared workspace pool; Perplexity Enterprise gives each seat 500 (Pro) or 15,000 (Max) credits then a shared org pool.
7. **Publish a prepay discount ladder.** Microsoft: 300,000 credits → **5%** off rising to 300,000,000 → **20%**; Agent P3 gives 5%/10%/15% at 20,000/100,000/500,000 ACUs, with **1 ACU = $1 = 100 Copilot Credits**.
8. **Make base credits exactly equal the price.** GitHub Copilot: **$10 → 1,000 base credits, $39 → 3,900, $100 → 10,000**, all at 1 credit = $0.01, with a variable "flex" allotment on top.
9. **Zero-rate a whole surface when the end user is licensed.** In Copilot Studio, classic answers, generative answers, agent actions, tenant graph grounding and agent-triggered flow runs cost "**No charge**" for Microsoft 365 Copilot-licensed users, and agents built in Copilot Studio for Teams consume **no credits at all**.
10. **Auto top-up with a hard monthly ceiling.** Perplexity auto-refill triggers at **500 credits (Pro) / 2,500 (Max)** with a user-set refill amount and monthly cap; ChatGPT auto-reload restores only to the chosen target balance up to a monthly maximum.
11. **A generous one-time bonus that expires fast.** Perplexity: Pro **4,000** and Max **35,000** bonus credits, both expiring in **30 days**; Enterprise Pro 8,000 and Enterprise Max 40,000.
12. **Publish API unit rates and per-request cost, even when the consumer meter is opaque.** xAI lists video at **$0.08/$0.14/$0.25 per second** and web search at **$5 per 1,000 calls**, and returns `cost_in_usd_ticks` in every API response — while consumer usage is only a percentage.
13. **Degrade gracefully instead of refusing.** Gemini falls back to a lighter model when a cap is hit and remembers your model choice across sessions; Grok drops 720p video to 480p once the tier's 720p cap is hit.
14. **Cheap verified-student and educator lanes.** Gemini AI Pro is free for 12 months for eligible U.S. students then $19.99/mo; Perplexity Education Pro is **$10/mo**; ChatGPT for Teachers is free through June 2027; GitHub Copilot Student is $0 with unlimited completions.

## 4. Patterns that frustrate users

1. **Percentage-only or multiplier-only meters with no absolute number.** Grok shows only "% used" of a weekly pool ("Total opacity… you would think they would provide some kind of pricing list but nah"); Claude publishes only relative multipliers ("at least 5x Free"); Gemini publishes 2x/4x/5x/20x on an unpublished compute quota.
2. **Silent mid-cycle allowance cuts at the same price.** ChatGPT Pro 200 goes from 20× to 10× the Plus allowance and GPT-6 Pro from 200 to 100 messages/week on 30 Oct 2026 for the same $200; xAI cut Grok caps in May 2026 (SuperGrok video ">20 videos per 24 hours", Heavy ">80 per 12 hours", image edits ~100/day → ~30/day) and changed "Near-unlimited usage" to "Highest usage limits".
3. **Metered actions that run with no pre-run cost estimate.** Microsoft: "Can I check the cost before running a task? **No.** Currently, you can only use `/cost` to check how many credits a task has already used"; OpenAI community: "The flow never mentioned credits, an estimated price, whether a free allowance…".
4. **Allowances that burn far faster than users expect.** Perplexity user: **~15,000 credits in under an hour** building a tool; Claude: **37% of a 5-hour session limit from a single CRUD prompt** and ~$100 of usage credits "washed away like in 30 mins"; GitHub Copilot: "I used 100% of copilot pro+ in just 2 days of normal work" and Max's 20,000 credits "burned through … in a matter of HOURS"; Grok Build users maxing a week in ~2 hours.
5. **Being charged for output you did not get.** xAI support confirmed **moderated or failed generations still count** toward the cap; Perplexity notes failed attempts may count as image generations; Gemini and xAI are the exceptions (failed requests never charged / Gemini caps per-prompt quota).
6. **You often cannot pay to continue on the meter you hit.** ChatGPT personal-plan credits cover only Codex/Work/Office apps, so a Chat, image, voice or deep-research limit has no purchase path and Support "cannot reset" limits; Perplexity video has "no option to purchase additional credits"; Claude monthly API credits cannot be spent on Claude Code or Cowork overage.
7. **Forfeiture and no rollover.** Perplexity: "All credits, monthly and purchased, are **forfeited when your subscription ends** and aren't refundable"; ChatGPT credits expire after 12 months and are non-refundable/non-transferable; Copilot Studio: "unused Copilot Credits don't carry over to the next month"; Microsoft P3 unused credits expire at the end of the annual term.
8. **Surprise pay-as-you-go bills.** Copilot Studio: "Woke up to a **$47K bill** after deploying ONE Copilot Studio agent", plus a separate thread reporting a ~$1,000/day PayGo baseline; on Copilot Cowork: "we all know how much finance and accounting departments love variable impacts on budgets".
9. **No family plan anywhere, and AI benefits that cannot be shared.** None of the six publishes a family plan; Microsoft 365 Family/Premium explicitly excludes Copilot ("AI features are available only to the subscription owner"); Claude project sharing is "No" on Free/Pro/Max; Perplexity and Grok credits are personal and non-transferable.
10. **Silent throttling at predictable times.** Claude drains 5-hour session limits faster during peak hours (weekdays 5–11am PT), resented as "paying the same price for less at predictable times of day"; Gemini non-subscribers may be blocked from Flow video generation entirely roughly 2 PM–5 PM UTC.
